description=[h2]How It Works[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Automatic swap on break[/b]
description=• Your character drops their broken spear immediately
description=• Next, they grab the spare on their back and equip it
description=• No spare on the back? you have a short window to press (R) and equip straight from inventory
description=
description=[b]Restock on the move[/b]
description=• Press (R) while holding a spear to mount a spear on your back
description=• Your character will use spears from inventory before going to spears in their bags
description=
description=[b]Stake the ground (Build 42)[/b]
description=Spears are heavy and slow the character down. Stake reserves ahead of the fight
description=• Right-click soft ground (dirt, sand, clay, grass) → [b]Stake Spear[/b]
description=• Kite through the staked line and rearm on the move
description=• Staked spears auto-equip when you contact them with empty hands.
description=• Zombies can knock staked spears flat into normal ground spears
description=• Requires vanilla [b]3D Ground Item[/b] (on by default)
description=
description=[hr]
description=
description=[h2]Spearbreaker Trait (6 points)[/h2]
description=• +2 Spear XP, +1 Carving XP
description=• Fire Harden Spear from day one
description=• Faster spear attacks (shorter delay between swings)
