description=[h2]How It Works[/h2]
description=
description=[b]When your spear breaks, the swap is automatic[/b]
description=• The broken shaft drops to the ground on its own — no menus, no pause
description=• You grab the spare from your back and keep fighting
description=• Nothing on your back? Press (R) quickly to equip a new spear from your inventory
description=
description=[b]Restock without breaking stride[/b]
description=• While holding a spear, press (R) to attach a spare from your inventory to your back
description=• Even digs them out of your bags for you
description=
description=[b]Stake your ground (Build 42)[/b]
description=Spears are heavy. Haul five into a fight and the horde catches you. Plant your reserves where the fight will happen instead — and walk in light.
description=• Right-click soft ground (dirt, sand, clay — grass too) → [b]Stake Spear[/b]
description=• Kite the horde through your line of staked spears and rearm on the move
description=• Walk over your stake with empty hands — it snaps straight into your grip
description=• Zombies that walk the square knock your stakes flat — then it's just a spear on the ground
description=• Needs the vanilla [b]3D Ground Item[/b] option (on by default) for the upright look
description=

description=• Reduced cooldown between spear attacks
